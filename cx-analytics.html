Customer experience · Data analytics · Power BI

Make customer feedback easier to act on.

A dashboard concept for bringing NPS, CSAT and CES into a clear, connected view—so teams can explore feedback across services and customer journeys.

Concept project · Illustrative data only · No company or customer information shown.

The problem space

Many signals, one service experience.

Customer feedback can arrive through different surveys, channels and moments. This concept focuses on making measures easier to scan together while keeping service context visible.

NPS

Review recommendation sentiment as one signal in the wider customer relationship.

CSAT

Compare satisfaction feedback with the service or touchpoint it relates to.

CES

Explore how easy customers felt it was to complete a task or resolve a need.

Design approach

From measurement to a useful conversation.

Keep each score distinct, connect it to the journey it describes, and make follow-up investigation straightforward.

Clear metric hierarchy

Keep NPS, CSAT and CES visible as distinct measures instead of blending them into one score.

Context with the number

Pair each indicator with its service, channel, time period and feedback source.

Readable trends

Use simple charts to prompt investigation, then move toward the feedback behind the pattern.

This is a portfolio concept. The dashboard visuals are illustrative placeholders, not actual company, customer, or project results. No business impact is claimed and this is not a deployed Power BI dashboard.
