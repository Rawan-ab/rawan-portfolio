A quieter way to explore wellness

TAWAZON brings wellness programs and curated collections into one responsive website. I framed the design challenge around helping people exploring yoga and mindful living scan the offers and find a clear next step. The work is a concept exploration; the audience and direction have not been validated through user research.

04 — DESIGN DECISIONS

Designed to make the next step visible

These are choices made in the concept—not findings from user testing.

01 · COLOR

Keep the page grounded

Slate blue anchors headings and key actions; warm gold appears on highlights; cream and mist soften the surrounding surfaces. The palette makes actions easy to spot while preserving open space.

02 · CONTENT

Let visitors scan before choosing

Collections and wellness programs are presented as distinct groups. Program cards pair an image with a short description, price, and action so the offer is legible without opening another screen.

03 · RESPONSIVE

Recompose for each screen

Separate desktop, tablet, and mobile Figma frames show the same visual direction adapting to narrower layouts, instead of simply shrinking the desktop canvas.

05 — PAGE FLOW

07 — REFLECTION

A useful starting point, with room to prove the flow

This concept brings a clear visual direction and responsive layouts together. The next iteration should test whether people can distinguish collections from programs, then design and evaluate the missing detail and cart steps. No usability outcomes are claimed.
