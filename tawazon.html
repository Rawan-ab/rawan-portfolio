A quieter way to explore wellness

TAWAZON brings wellness programs and curated collections into one responsive website. This concept focuses on the visual language, page composition, and interface details shown in the design.

Slate blue, warm gold, and soft neutrals carry the visual identity. Purple is used here for portfolio navigation and case study accents, while the product screens retain their original colors.
