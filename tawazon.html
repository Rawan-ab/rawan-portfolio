02 — COLOR LANGUAGE

Soft contrast, grounded by blue

Slate blue anchors the experience, warm gold draws attention to key actions, and soft neutrals give the interface room to breathe.

SLATE BLUE#55798APrimary actions
WARM GOLD#DFB85BHighlights
CREAM#F5EFE4Warm backgrounds
MIST#DCE7E8Soft surfaces
DEEP BLUE#3B5360Headings and emphasis
Color directionSlate blue keeps the interface grounded; warm gold helps important actions stand out without making the page feel busy.
