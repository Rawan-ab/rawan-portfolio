Outcome

Defined the reader journey and translated it into an end-to-end product concept, including information architecture, mobile interfaces and companion seller tools.

01 · The opportunity

Reading is personal. The tools around it are scattered.

The project explores how one experience could bring reading goals, personal libraries, reader communities, nearby places and literary events closer together.

The design challenge

How might we help readers stay engaged and discover more ways to enjoy reading?

Project framing from the UX analysis: make progress visible, support social reading, and surface bookstores and events in one place.

Reading goals can be difficult to follow over time.
Reviews, reader conversations and local events can live in separate services.
Finding a nearby bookshop or literary event takes extra effort.
Design persona from project brief

Sara Al-Faisal

27 · Marketing specialist · Riyadh

Enjoys fiction and cultural events, but wants an easier way to keep a reading routine and connect with fellow readers.

06 · Reflection

A broad concept that needs a focused first release.

Reading2Gather brings together several product areas. The next step would be to test whether the main reading journey feels clear before expanding the marketplace and seller tools.

What this project establishes

A product direction, personas, competitor review, information architecture, screen requirements and interface concepts for readers and sellers.

What I would validate next

Test navigation and the core flow from discovering a book to saving it, tracking progress and finding a relevant group or event. No user-test results are claimed here.
